The three problems

parser: day and month flipped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. It covers every tenant and every channel except the booking-v3 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says and the date flips. Only tenants on booking-v3, only through the form. The parser fix does not touch it: the fix is the hint.

shift: the date moves by one day. These reports are not a flip. Tuesday 6 October shows as Monday; a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday; an invite arrives before the booking was made. That reads as a time-zone or offset bug in how the invite date is built. The desk spotted these (desk notes) and filed them under parser by tenant; they stay counted there and are counted again here.
